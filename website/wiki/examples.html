Running an example

From the terminal:

cmc run examples/15_fizzbuzz.cmc

From OGABOOGA CODER: click Examples, pick one, and it opens in the Text tab. Press RUN.

Suggested order for a new coder

  1. 01, 02, 04, 06 (talking, boxes, choices, loops)
  2. 08, 11, 12 (piles and text)
  3. 09, 10 (clumps)
  4. 15, 19, 21 (classic puzzles)
  5. 26, 22 (recursion and text surgery)
  6. 17, 27, 30 (drawing)
  7. 16, 25 (games -- give them to a friend)

Writing your own examples

The best way to learn is to change an example until it breaks, then fix it. Try:

  • Change every message to something silly.
  • Make a loop run 100 times.
  • Add a clump that does something the example does not have.
  • Draw something from your own room with skrib.
