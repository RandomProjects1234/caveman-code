All the CMC words

Every word in the language, one page each. Start with the ones in Talking and Boxes.

Talking

  • blorp (ask) - Ask the user a question and get their answer as text.
  • oga (print) - Say something on the screen.

Boxes

  • grunk (let) - Make a box and put something in it.

Choices

  • binga (if) - Do something only when a check is gronk.
  • unga (end) - Close a block of code.
  • wonga (else) - What to do when the binga check was not gronk.

Logic

  • and - Gronk only when both sides are gronk.
  • not - Flip gronk to nork and nork to gronk.
  • or - Gronk when at least one side is gronk.

Loops

  • booga (repeat) - Do something again and again, a set number of times.
  • in - Am I inside this pile / text? Also used by zoop.
  • zoop (for) - Do something once for each thing in a pile or text.
  • zug (while) - Keep doing something while a check stays gronk.

Clumps

  • clump (fn) - Make your own word that does a bunch of things.
  • ork (return) - Give an answer back from a clump.

Values

  • gronk (true) - The truth value for YES.
  • nork (false) - The truth value for NO.
  • pi - The circle number, about 3.14159.
  • plop (nothing) - Nothing at all. An empty spot.

Piles

  • nom - How many things? (length of a pile or text)
  • plop - Plop a thing onto the end of a pile.
  • skoop - Scoop out one thing from a pile or text by its spot number.
  • snorf - Make a pile of things.
  • yoink - Take the last thing off a pile.

Text

  • find - Where does a smaller text start inside a bigger text?
  • flip - Turn text backwards.
  • goop - Squish anything into text.
  • join - Stick a pile of things together into one text.
  • shout - Make text ALL CAPS.
  • split - Chop text into a pile of pieces.
  • what - Ask what kind of thing something is.
  • whisper - Make text all small letters.

Numbers

  • abs - How far from zero? (make negative numbers positive)
  • big - The bigger of two numbers.
  • flat - Smash a decimal down to the whole number below it.
  • munga - Magic random number between two numbers.
  • numba - Turn text into a number.
  • pow - A number times itself many times.
  • roof - Lift a decimal up to the whole number above it.
  • root - The square root: what number times itself makes this?
  • round - Make a decimal into the closest whole number.
  • small - The smaller of two numbers.

Drawing

  • skrib (draw) - Draw on the picture window (OGABOOGA CODER's Draw tab).
  • wait - Pause the program for some milliseconds.

Notes

  • ugg (comment) - A note for humans. The computer ignores it.
